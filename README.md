Play as a cat that hates the ocean as disgusting filthy crabs try to invade the land. Each day, the crabs get closer and closer to the shore and none of the stupid humans know of their oncoming threat. It is up to you, Catticus, to defend the world...they also said if you survive 5 days they'll be chill again for some reason. Get various upgrades in between each wave to increase your chances of repelling the vile crabs.
